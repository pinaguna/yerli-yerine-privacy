The App does not collect, store, or transmit personal information or sensitive personal information.

Because the App does not collect personal information, we do not use personal information for advertising, profiling, marketing, analytics, personalization, or any other purpose.

The App may use local device storage for information required for the operation of the game, such as game progress or settings, where applicable.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Because the App does not require an account and does not collect personal information, users can play the game without providing personal information to us.

8. Data Security

Since the App does not collect or transmit personal information to our systems, we do not maintain a server-side database of users' personal information.

Users should nevertheless keep their devices and operating systems reasonably protected and up to date.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not retain personal information because the App does not collect personal information.

Any game-related information stored locally on the device is controlled by the device and the App's local storage. Users can generally remove such locally stored data by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App, subject to the behavior of the operating system.
